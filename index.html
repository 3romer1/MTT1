FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is a fractional CIO and how can it help my business?

A fractional (virtual) CIO provides senior IT leadership on an advisory basis — owning your technology roadmap, aligning IT spend to business goals, and reporting to leadership, without the cost of a full-time executive hire.

Do you provide on-site services in Collin County and Orange County?

Yes. We're headquartered in Frisco, TX, serving Collin County including McKinney, Plano, Allen, and Prosper, with a California presence in Irvine serving Orange County. Remote services are available nationwide.

What cybersecurity services do you provide?

Security assessments, penetration testing, firewall management, endpoint detection and response, identity and access management, compliance alignment (HIPAA, SOC 2, PCI DSS, CMMC), virtual CISO services, and incident response planning.

How does MTT approach AI adoption?

Govern first, deploy second. We assess your data and infrastructure readiness, build governance frameworks aligned to NIST AI RMF and the EU AI Act, deploy tools securely, and train your workforce — so adoption is measurable from day one.

How is MTT different from a typical managed services provider?

Most MSPs focus on reactive tickets. We start with business strategy — understanding your revenue model and compliance obligations before recommending technology — and measure success by business outcomes, not tickets closed.

How does the procurement service work?

We act as a single procurement partner for all IT purchases, leveraging vendor relationships for volume pricing typically reserved for larger organizations — from requirements analysis through warranty management.
