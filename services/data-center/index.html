Data Center Buildout & Support

What's included

Design & Capacity Planning

Rack layout, power and cooling requirements, cable pathways, and growth headroom — planned before the first rack arrives.

Rack & Stack Buildout

Racking, cabling, labeling, and documentation done to standard — servers, switches, storage, and PDUs installed clean and verified.

Structured Cabling

Copper and fiber runs, patch panel builds, and cable management that stays serviceable years later.

Migrations & Consolidations

Equipment moves, colo-to-colo transitions, and consolidations planned around your uptime requirements.

Smart Hands Services

On-site technicians acting as your remote hands: reboots, device swaps, cable moves, media handling, and troubleshooting — dispatched so your staff doesn't have to be.

Ongoing Support & Maintenance

Scheduled maintenance, break-fix coordination, vendor escorting, and documented site visits.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What are smart hands services?

On-site technicians who act as your team's remote hands inside the data center — performing physical tasks like reboots, hardware swaps, cabling changes, and visual inspections under your direction, without you sending staff on-site.

Do you work in colocation facilities?

Yes — we perform buildouts, migrations, and smart hands work in colocation and on-premises data centers across Texas and California.

Can you handle a complete data center migration?

Yes — from inventory and planning through cutover, with downtime windows agreed in advance and equipment verified at the destination.
