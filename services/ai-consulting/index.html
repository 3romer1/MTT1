FAQ

Frequently asked questions

We're not a tech company — is AI relevant to us?

Almost certainly. The highest-ROI uses are often mundane: drafting documents, summarizing meetings, automating data entry, and speeding up customer response. We start where the payoff is fastest.

How do we keep our data safe when using AI tools?

We deploy business-grade AI platforms with proper access controls, data-handling boundaries, and usage policies — so sensitive information stays protected and compliant.

Do we need to hire AI specialists?

No. That's the point of enablement — we set up the tools, build the workflows, and train your existing team to run them.

Where do we start?

With a readiness assessment. In a short engagement we identify your top opportunities, estimate the payoff, and map a phased rollout.
